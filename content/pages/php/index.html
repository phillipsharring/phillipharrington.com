Web Nerdism w/ PHP 101

How the web works, taught with PHP. Short lessons, one page each, then small projects that use them. It starts before PHP, with how two computers talk at all, and it ends with you building things that hold up when someone tries to break them.

It's being written now, a lesson at a time. Linked lessons are up; the rest are on their way.
