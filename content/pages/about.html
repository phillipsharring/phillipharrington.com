About

I'm a software team lead who plays drums. Or a drummer who leads software teams, depending on the day. Back in the 7th grade somebody called me a "real band rat" who'd play with anyone, any time, and that's pretty much been my career ever since: join the band, find the groove, and keep good time so everybody else can shine.

Work has the software side, Music has the bands, gear and history, and Now has what I'm up to lately.

How I Got to Smithfield

I grew up out Californeeway, mostly in Watsonville, and studied music at Cabrillo College in Aptos before heading east to Berklee in Boston, briefly. Then came Florida, for a job opportunity, and then a couple of years in Arizona, where I got to reconnect with my father. Then it was back to Florida for a few more years. In 2013 work brought me to North Carolina, and I've lived, loved and learned here ever since. I bounced around Raleigh & Durham, and now I'm in Smithfield, y'all.
