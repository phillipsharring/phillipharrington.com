Music

I've played drums almost my entire life. I studied theory, ear training, arranging, composition, performance & songwriting at Berklee College of Music in Boston, MA and Cabrillo Community College in Aptos, CA.

In the 7th grade I was described as a "real band rat, who will play with anyone, any time." This turned out to be prophetic.

I love music, especially playing rock music. Being on stage w/ the band between all the amps and monitors playing drums is to literally be at the center of the music, both sonically and physically because you can feel every note in your body. It's an amazing experience.

Everyone can and should play drums. Yes, I mean everyone!

You can hear some samples of my drumming on tracks from different projects on my On Drums, party people, It's Mr. Phil Harrington playlist.

“The best thing for drummers is to have fun.”

    I've written a bunch of songs and collaborated on a ton more, and I've done open mics on guitar and vocals. I've been a registered writer with B.M.I. since 1994, and I've received actual royalty checks!

    Sticks

    Vic Firth SD2 Boleros since I was 11 or 12. I'd been using their swizzle sticks (basically an SD2 with a mallet on the butt) and went looking for just the stick.

    Audio

    A Rolls PM351 personal monitor in a small rack with Shure in-ears, a battered SM57 (58? Something like that.) and an iPad for the click. The upshot: I can turn up my own vocal without begging the monitor mix, so I stay in tune.

    TAMA Swingstar. Black, "accel" depths: 12″, 13″ and 16″ toms, a 22″ × 18″ kick (way too deep) and a basic snare. The 8″ and 10″ toms came later as a pair. Lives on as Baby Drum Set.
