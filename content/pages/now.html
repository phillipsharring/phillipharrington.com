Full House

We've mostly adjusted to life with the full house, since the Goldsboro family had to move in. It's been over two and a half years now. We traded in the truck for a mini-van that can hold everyone, and traded in my super sexy sports car for a Jeep.

It's a little hectic at times, but we've settled into a routine and having the family around is really nice. We try to have meals around our giant table several times a week and that's been lovely.

The (now) 10-year-old did baseball summer camps, took lessons and played in the summer league as catcher, a position he has grown to love. We took him to see Shohei Ohtani hit 3 home runs and strike out 10 in the Dodgers' NLCS Game 4 clincher against the Brewers. He made the team again this year and his skills and team spirit are really improving.

AND, he's playing drums now, too. And so is his little brother, who's two and a half. Awesome.

Yes, I'm Still Playing Drums!
